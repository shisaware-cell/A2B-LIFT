How daily lift club works

A weekday commute you can plan around.

Daily Lift Club is for people who travel to the same work areas on weekdays and want a stable, verified car instead of searching for transport every morning. Search your pickup area, choose your workplace area, then compare cars, driver verification, pricing, and remaining seats.

When you book, your pass covers weekdays only. Weekly passes cover 5 workdays. Monthly passes cover weekday commute access for the selected period. Weekends are excluded by design.
